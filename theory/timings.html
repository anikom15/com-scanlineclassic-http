Estimated Timings of Popular Systems

Consoles

Generation 4

Super Famicom (Super Nintendo Entertainment System)

NTSC
All Modes
  • Chroma subcarrier frequency – 3.579545 MHz (fsc)
  • Pixel clock – 21.47727 MHz (Fs=6fsc)
  • Clock cycles per line – 1364, except for line 240 (Nh)
  • Horizontal frequency – 15.74580 kHz, except for line 240 (fh=FsNh)
Non-Interlaced Modes
  • Line 240, clock cycles – 1360 (Nh,240)
  • Line 240, horizontal frequency – 15.79211 Hz (fh,240=FsNh,240)
  • Lines per field – 262 (Nv)
  • Clock cycles per field - 357 364 (N=Nh(Nv-1)+Nh,240)
  • Field rate – 60.09914 Hz (fv=FsN)
Interlaced Modes
  • Line 240, clock cycles – 1364 (Nh)
  • Line 240, horizontal frequency – 15.79211 kHz (fh)
  • Lines per field – 263 even, 262 odd (Nv0,Nv1)
  • Clock cycles per field – 358 732 even, 357 368 odd (N0,N1)
  • Clock cycles per frame – 716 100 (N0+1)
  • Field rate – 59.86996 Hz even, 60.09847 Hz odd (fv0=FsN0,fv1=FsN1)
  • Frame rate – 29.99110 Hz (fv=FsN0+1)
Output Modes
  • 256x224@60p
  • 256x239@60p
  • 512x224@60p
  • 256x448@60i
  • 512x239@60p
  • 256x478@60i
  • 512x448@60i
  • 512x478@60i
