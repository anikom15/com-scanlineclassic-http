Estimated Timings of Popular Systems

Conventions used in the following lists:

  • Lines start at 1, not 0
  • Field subscripts 0 and 1 denote even and odd fields, respectively
  • In NTSC, the even field is considered the first field of a frame, and the odd field is the second
  • In PAL, the odd field is considered the first field of a frame, and the even field is the second

Consoles

Generation 4

Super Famicom (Super Nintendo Entertainment System)

NTSC
All Modes
  • Chroma subcarrier frequency – 3.579545 MHz (fsc)
  • Pixel clock – 21.47727 MHz (Fs=6fsc)
  • Normal clock cycles per line – 1364 (see line 241 below) (Nh)
  • Normal horizontal frequency – 15.74580 kHz (see line 241 below) (fh=FsNh)
Non-Interlaced Modes
  • Line 241, clock cycles – 1364 even, 1360 odd (Nh,Nh1,241)
  • Line 241, horizontal frequency – 15.74580 kHz even, 15.79211 kHz odd (fh,fh1,241=FsNh1,241)
  • Lines per field – 262 (Nv)
  • Clock cycles per field – 357 368 even, 357 364 odd (N0=NhNv,N1=Nh(Nv-1)+Nh1,241)
  • Field rate – 60.09847 Hz even, 60.09914 Hz odd (fv0=FsN0,fv1=FsN1)
Interlaced Modes
  • Line 241, clock cycles – 1364 (Nh)
  • Line 241, horizontal frequency – 15.74580 kHz (fh)
  • Lines per field – 263 even, 262 odd (Nv0,Nv1)
  • Clock cycles per field – 358 732 even, 357 368 odd (N0=NhNv0,N1=NhNv1)
  • Clock cycles per frame – 716 100 (N0+1=N0+N1)
  • Field rate – 59.86996 Hz even, 60.09847 Hz odd (fv0=FsN0,fv1=FsN1)
  • Frame rate – 29.99200 Hz (fframe=FsN0+1)
Output Modes
  • 256x224@60p
  • 256x239@60p
  • 512x224@60p
  • 256x448@60i
  • 512x239@60p
  • 256x478@60i
  • 512x448@60i
  • 512x478@60i
PAL
All Modes
  • Chroma subcarrier frequency – 4.433618750 MHz (fsc)
  • Pixel clock – 21.28137000 MHz (Fs=245fsc)
  • Normal clock cycles per line – 1364 (see line 312 below) (Nh)
  • Normal horizontal frequency – 15.60217742 kHz (see line 312 below) (fh=FsNh)
Non-Interlaced Modes
  • Line 312, clock cycles – 1364 (Nh)
  • Line 312, horizontal frequency – 15.60217742 kHz (fh)
  • Lines per field – 312 (Nv)
  • Clock cycles per field – 425 568 (N=NhNv)
  • Field rate – 50.00697891 Hz (fv=FsN)
Interlaced Modes
  • Line 312, clock cycles – 1368 odd, 1364 even (Nh1,312,Nh)
  • Line 312, horizontal frequency – 15.55655702 kHz odd, 15.60217742 kHz even (fh1,312=FsNh1,312,fh)
  • Lines per field – 312 odd, 313 even (Nv1,Nv0)
  • Clock cycles per field – 425 572 odd, 426 932 even (N1=Nh(Nv1-1)+Nh1,312,N0=NhNv0)
  • Clock cycles per frame – 852 504 (N1+0=N1+N0)
  • Field rate – 50.00650889 Hz odd, 49.84721220 Hz even (fv1=FsN1,fv0=FsN0)
  • Frame rate – 24.96336674 Hz (fframe=FsN1+0)
Output Modes

  • 256x239@50p
  • 512x224@50p
  • 256x448@50i
  • 512x239@50p
  • 256x478@50i
  • 512x448@50i
  • 512x478@50i
