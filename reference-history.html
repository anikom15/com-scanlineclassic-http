History of Scanline Classic

Scanline Classic originally began as a modification to the Catmull-Rom shader, which was part of the RetroArch shader collection, to have scanlines that could dynamically support arbitrary resolutions and interlacing. This was followed by the "Scanline Advanced" pipeline which simulated a variety of CRT geometry effects and phosphor simulation. Over time, the project evolved to incorporate a more comprehensive approach to simulating classic display characteristics, leading to the development of further pipelines. The current pipeline in use today is referred to as New DSP and represents the current functionality of the project.
