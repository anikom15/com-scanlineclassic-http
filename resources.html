Shader development outside Scanline Classic

CRT and Display Simulation

  • crt-beans Experimental CRT shader project exploring alternative beam and image formation models.
  • crt-guest A widely used CRT shader family focused on practical image quality, flexibility, and community-driven development.
  • Megatron An HDR-focused CRT shader emphasizing physically motivated display simulation and a restrained design philosophy.

All-in-One Shader Systems

  • koko-aio A configurable all-in-one shader framework combining multiple display and video simulation techniques.

Handheld and LCD Simulation

  • Pokefan531's Handheld Shaders Handheld LCD shaders informed by hardware research, display measurements, and color characterization.

Preset Collections and Research Projects

  • CyberLab Presets System-specific CRT presets developed through extensive comparison and testing on a variety of displays.
  • PlainOldPants's Shaders Display-focused shaders exploring system-specific presentation, color, and CRT characteristics.

Historical Shaders

  • Mega Bezel A comprehensive display simulation framework that extends beyond the screen to recreate the complete viewing environment.
  • MAME HLSL A CRT simulation shader developed for the MAME project.
  • crt-royale A pioneering high-fidelity CRT shader that served as a reference point for future development.
  • Lottes CRT A classic CRT shader known for its simplicity, performance, and public domain status.
